Primary & Secondary Market MOCK

Built for Brazil's play market — soccer, music, and large tech conventions — with secondary sales and partner referrals across Brazil, Central America, and the Caribbean.

Every ticket is an NFT on LightLink (EVM), held in the buyer's own wallet — no affiliate account needed. At mint, the sale is sealed with an REE receipt and a TEE attestation that the ticket was issued by UZEmarkets — so any later buyer can verify the ticket is genuine and trace the original NFT from the first buyer's wallet to theirs. Resale moves the same NFT; the provenance chain is the fraud check. Matching itself stays on NEAR: buyers and sellers post intents, and the solver network matches the parties — matching is the marketplace's core function, and solvers are how it scales. Wallet and chain are mocked here; the production integration is with our Australian partner Tickify.io on LightLink (founder Roy Hui, participating affiliate partner).

Where the margin is: we enable tickets at the original (commercial) price — but the real margin is the hold. A buyer with launch-market access to a big-name event can secure more than one ticket at the commercial price and resell into the secondary premium — the price people with money pay to choose exactly where they sit. Two prices, one attested NFT the whole way. Your tickets below show both: what you paid at launch, and what the secondary market says now.

📈 UZE Event Market — Call It MOCK

For big fixtures, the market itself becomes the game. Call the price Tribuna Central (Lower Ring) will settle at in the secondary market by match week for Internacional vs São Paulo FC. When the market settles, the two closest calls win tickets: closest call takes the grand prize — a PAIR of tickets; second closest takes the consolation — a SINGLE ticket. Prize tickets are minted as the same attested NFTs, straight into the winner's wallet. Prize inventory is held back from the platform's own primary allocation \u2014 never bought off the secondary market, so nobody can pump the index to inflate a prize. Calls are open to primary ticket holders only: you must own the asset to price its secondary market. Every call is a signal the supply side prices against — demand, revealed before on-sale. Positions bridge in via NEAR Intents (mocked here).
